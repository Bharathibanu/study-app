# Integrated Mental Maths Masterclass

The uploaded masterclass is integrated into **Maths Tricks**, together with the app’s existing multiplication material. Duplicate methods are combined, not repeated as a separate full course.

## Five-level roadmap

1. **Foundations:** splitting numbers, left-to-right multiplication, repeated doubling, doubling/halving and powers-of-ten basics.
2. **Special multipliers:** ×5, ×9, ×11 (including three-digit carrying), ×12, ×15, ×20, ×25, ×50, ×75, ×99, ×101, ×125, ×999 and ×1001.
3. **Number patterns:** base100 below/above the base, base1000 with leading zeros, squares ending in5, difference of squares, square expansion and the same-tens/complementary-units pattern.
4. **Advanced multiplication:** compensation, factorization, two-/three-digit crosswise multiplication and chunking4-/5-digit numbers. The number of diagonal stages for equal-length n-digit multiplication is2n−1; mentally, a simpler factorization can be preferable.
5. **SSC speed training:** estimation with exact verification, square memory, exact fraction multipliers, method selection and timed practice.

The general base rule also explains why base10,100 and1000 reserve one, two and three right-hand digits. No shortcut is universally fastest; the method chooser suggests a suitable route, not an optimality guarantee.

## What was merged

Existing doubling/halving, ×5/25/125, ×9/99, ×11, near100, squares ending in5, difference-of-squares and crosswise sections now include the uploaded course’s additional worked examples. Trick22 repeats Trick17’s condition and is merged into the same-tens/units-sum10 section. Matching first digits alone is explicitly insufficient.

Twelve missing methods are added inside the matching existing levels, each with visual steps, a worked model question, a solution and applicability limits. The13 SSC topic methods added earlier remain available below the course.

Existing numbered mastery flags are retained. They are personal flags; newly expanded content should be reviewed before treating a previously marked level as fully mastered.

## Practice and recording

Each level’s **Try5questions** now draws from the expanded methods rather than the old limited generator. On completing the set, question attempts and per-question time feed Topic Practice, and wrong answers feed the Mistake Notebook. Retry creates a new attempt set.

The **10-question timed test** uses a stopwatch, not a hard countdown. The pasted document contains only Question1 (99×98), despite announcing ten questions. That question and its options are retained; nine additional model questions were written from the teaching examples. These are **not official SSC PYQs**.

Press Check answer/choose an option, then Next question, and finish the set to save the attempt records. Leaving before completion does not save a partial set. Timings measure elapsed time while the question is present, including pauses; they are not an official exam-performance assessment.

## Reference tools

- **Method chooser:** positive whole factors from1 to999999, a suggested method with conditions, and the exact product.
- **Square memory map:** computed squares11–50 and a link to the full square drill.
- **Fraction reference:** exact decimal equivalents of1/2,1/4,1/5,1/8,1/10,1/20 and1/25.
- **Seven-day practice checklist:** the uploaded seven-day roadmap, with completion stored in your account. It is independent of the190-day SSC timetable and awards no study minutes automatically.

The original tables12–30, individual/mixed drills and flashcards are retained.
